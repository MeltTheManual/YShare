Quick connect server
YShare has no server of its own. Nothing you send passes through us. The 6-character code needs a small connect-me service to introduce the two devices. Run your own (see SELF-HOSTING.md) or paste one you trust. Leave it empty to use Manual Connect without a Quick Connect server. Without TURN, the devices still need a direct path.
